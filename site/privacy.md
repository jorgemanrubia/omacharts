Omacharts is a desktop application. It runs entirely on your computer, and
nothing about you or how you use it is collected, sent or stored anywhere
else. There are no accounts, no telemetry and no servers of ours.

**Your data stays on your machine.** Watchlists, chartbooks, drawings,
templates, settings and the market data cache live in
`~/.local/share/omacharts`, and nowhere else.

**Market data comes straight from the provider you choose.** To draw a
chart, Omacharts asks that provider for prices, from your computer: Yahoo
Finance by default, or thinkorswim if you sign in to it. What those requests
carry is the symbols you chart. Those providers' own privacy policies cover
what they do with them. Signing in to thinkorswim happens on thinkorswim's
own page, where you type your password yourself: Omacharts never sees it,
and keeps only the session that results, in a file on your computer.

**This website** is a set of static pages served by GitHub Pages. It sets no
cookies and has no analytics. GitHub may keep the logs any web server keeps,
as described in the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The pages load the Archivo typeface from Google Fonts.

**Videos.** Omacharts' videos are published on its YouTube channel by its
author, with a tool that uses YouTube API Services to manage that one
channel. It does not access, collect or store data about viewers or anyone
else. YouTube's own terms apply to watching them: the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Questions go to the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/jorgemanrubia/omacharts/issues).
