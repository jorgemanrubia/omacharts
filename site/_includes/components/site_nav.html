{%- comment -%} The manual's table of contents, in three tiers: the letter on its own at the top, then each section from site.manual.sections with the chapters that name it in `section:`, in nav_order. A chapter can have pages beneath it, and they pages of their own: a page under manual// says `parent: `, one under manual/// says `parent: /`, each with its own nav_order. _plugins/manual.rb hangs them on the chapter as manual_children. They are listed indented, a level open while it or a page under it is being read (manual_ancestors), and behind its chevron otherwise (the theme's own expander, which its script toggles). The levels are components/nav_children.html, which includes itself for the next.
