The volume indicators show how much traded: bar by bar and at each price. Who was buying and who was selling is [order flow](/manual/indicators/order-flow/). The market profile sits with them, showing how long the price spent at each price. Volume and the volume profile are drawn as bars and have no thickness or line style.
