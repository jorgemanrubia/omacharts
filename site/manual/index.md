I've always struggled with charting software making me choose. Do you want ease of use or do you want features? Do you want convenience or do you want power? Do you want beautiful charts and annotations or do you want the real deal?

Omacharts is my answer to those dilemmas. I want all the good stuff, all the time, without compromises.

### Beautiful, fast charts

Charts are the epicenter. You should be able to stop and admire how good they look, annotate them effortlessly, and browse through them as fast as your mind moves.

Beauty isn't just decoration. When you spend hours looking at charts, every little detail matters. Typography, colors, spacing, rendering, and the way things move all contribute to the experience.

And speed is non-negotiable. Panning, zooming, switching symbols, changing timeframes, and navigating history should feel instantaneous. The software should never get in the way of your train of thought.

I want charts that are a pleasure to look at and a joy to work with.

### Designed for power users, gentle with newcomers

Powerful software has a tendency to make you pay for that power with complexity. Endless menus, intimidating dialogs, obscure settings, and interfaces that seem designed to test your patience.

I don't think it has to be that way.

Omacharts is designed to satisfy demanding users without making newcomers feel like they've walked into an airplane cockpit.

The basics should be obvious. Common operations should be effortless. Advanced capabilities should be there when you need them, without getting in the way when you don't.

And as you become more proficient, the software should reward you. Keyboard shortcuts, efficient workflows, and deeper controls should let you operate it at a completely different level.

### Strong defaults, extreme configurability

I love opinionated software. I want someone to have thought deeply about the details and made good decisions on my behalf.

I don't want to spend my first hour configuring a charting application before I can enjoy using it. I want to open it and find something beautiful and useful right away.

But I also know that charting is deeply personal. Everyone has their own preferences, workflows, and peculiarities. And the more time you spend with a tool, the more particular you become about how it should work.

So Omacharts starts with strong opinions but doesn't force you to live with them.

Colors, styles, layouts, shortcuts, behaviors, and workflows should be yours to shape. You should be able to make the software feel like your own, without fighting it.

The defaults are a carefully curated starting point, not a cage.

### Agents-ready

Software is entering a new era, and I want Omacharts to embrace it from the beginning.

For decades, using sophisticated software meant learning how to operate its interface. You had to discover what was possible, learn where things lived, and master the steps required to get things done.

AI agents change that relationship.

I want Omacharts to be a tool that agents can operate as naturally as humans do. Not by awkwardly clicking through menus, but through well-defined capabilities that let them interact with the application directly.

Imagine asking an agent to prepare a workspace for studying a particular market, configure a set of charts, apply your preferred indicators, or help you investigate a trading idea.

Instead of teaching you which buttons to press, the agent can do the work.

This doesn't make a good human interface any less important. Quite the opposite. I want Omacharts to be delightful to operate yourself, while also being ready for a world where you can delegate work to an intelligent assistant.

---

That's the bar I'm aiming for.
