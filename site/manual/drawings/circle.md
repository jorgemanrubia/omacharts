An ellipse that fills the box between two corners.

| Key | Placing it | CLI |
|---|---|---|
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>C</kbd> | Press at one corner, release at the other, as for a [rectangle](/manual/drawings/rectangle/) | `ellipse`, `circle` |

## Properties

**Background**, **Transparency %**, **Border** and **Select by border only** on the **General** tab, and a label on the **Text** tab ([Properties](/manual/drawings/#properties)). A new circle is filled at 13% of its configuration's colour, with a 1 px edge of it ([Configurations](/manual/drawings/#configurations)).
