A level. It stays flat however you drag it and spans the time between its two ends.

| Key | Placing it | CLI |
|---|---|---|
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>H</kbd> | Placed as a [line](/manual/drawings/line/) | `hline` |

## Properties

**Colour**, **Thickness**, **Line style**, **Arrow** and **Arrowhead** on the **General** tab, and a label on the **Text** tab ([Properties](/manual/drawings/#properties)). A new level is 1.5 px with no arrow, in its configuration's colour ([Configurations](/manual/drawings/#configurations)).
