A line with a head at the end.

| Key | Placing it | CLI |
|---|---|---|
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>A</kbd> | Placed as a [line](/manual/drawings/line/); the head is at the end you place last | `arrow` |

## Properties

**Colour**, **Thickness**, **Line style**, **Arrow** and **Arrowhead** on the **General** tab, and a label on the **Text** tab ([Properties](/manual/drawings/#properties)). A new arrow is 1.5 px with an open head at the end, in its configuration's colour ([Configurations](/manual/drawings/#configurations)).
