---
title: "Zig-zag"
description: "A line through as many corners as you place"
section: charting
parent: drawings
nav_order: 5
---

<kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd>. Press at each corner. A second press on the last corner, a double-click or <kbd>Enter</kbd> ends it there; <kbd>Esc</kbd> drops it.

Its look is set in its [properties](/manual/drawings/#properties) and its [configurations](/manual/drawings/#configurations).
