A line through as many corners as you place.

| Key | Placing it | CLI |
|---|---|---|
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> | Press at each corner. A second press on the last corner, a double-click or <kbd>Enter</kbd> ends it there; <kbd>Esc</kbd> drops it | `zigzag` |

## Properties

**Colour**, **Thickness**, **Line style**, **Arrow** and **Arrowhead** on the **General** tab, and a label on the **Text** tab ([Properties](/manual/drawings/#properties)). A new zig-zag is 1.5 px with an open head at the end, in its configuration's colour ([Configurations](/manual/drawings/#configurations)).
