A box between two corners.

| Key | Placing it | CLI |
|---|---|---|
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>R</kbd> | Press at one corner, release at the other | `rect` |

## Properties

**Background**, **Transparency %**, **Border** and **Select by border only** on the **General** tab, and a label on the **Text** tab ([Properties](/manual/drawings/#properties)). A new rectangle is filled at 13% of its configuration's colour, with a 1 px edge of it ([Configurations](/manual/drawings/#configurations)).
